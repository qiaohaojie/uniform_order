# UniformOrder Launch Film Design

## Style Prompt

Warm editorial motion for an Australian school-community platform: the tactile authority of a well-designed paper order form, translated into a precise digital operations film. Use parchment-led layouts, deep institutional navy bookends, oxblood product accents, restrained gold rules, generous breathing room, and real product UI as the proof. Motion should feel confident and useful rather than flashy: page-like pushes for continuity, brief focus pulls for topic changes, and one decisive zoom reveal into the live product.

## Colors

- `#FAF6EE` — parchment canvas
- `#081A2D` — deep navy bookends and primary ink
- `#15212F` — body ink on light frames
- `#B08A3E` — restrained gold accent and rules
- `#8A1F2D` — oxblood product accent, derived from the Riverside Academy demo tenant

## Typography

- **Newsreader** — expressive editorial headlines and product statements
- **JetBrains Mono** — labels, route-like metadata, counts, and open-source technical notes
- Product screenshots retain the application typography unchanged.

## Motion

- Primary transition: editorial horizontal push, `0.4–0.5s`, `power3.inOut`
- Accent transition: vertical editorial push, `0.5s`, `power3.inOut`
- Entrances establish hierarchy with varied direction, scale, rule drawing, and letter-spacing.
- One ambient motion per scene; holds are intentional.
- No exit animation before scene transitions; final scene may fade to navy.

## What NOT to Do

- No neon, cyan-on-dark, glossy glassmorphism, or purple-blue gradients.
- No generic equal-card grids or centered floating text stacks.
- No invented product UI, stock photography, or unsupported roadmap claims.
- No kinetic clutter that competes with the real application screens.
- No pure black or pure white; use the tinted brand neutrals.
